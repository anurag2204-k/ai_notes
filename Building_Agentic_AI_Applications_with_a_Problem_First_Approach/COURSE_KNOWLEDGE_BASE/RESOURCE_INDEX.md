## 4. Landmark Research Papers

| Topic | Paper Title | Authors / Venue | ArXiv Link | Curriculum Role |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---|
| **Reasoning Prompting** | *Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models* | Wei et al. (NeurIPS 2022) | [arXiv:2201.11903](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11903) | Foundation for step-by-step reasoning |
| **Complex Reasoning** | *Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models* | Yao et al. (NeurIPS 2023) | [arXiv:2305.10601](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601) | Multi-branch heuristic search |
| **Agent Action & Reasoning** | *ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models* | Yao et al. (ICLR 2023) | [arXiv:2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) | Core agent execution loop for Assignment 3 |
| **Self-Verification** | *Self-Consistency Improves Chain of Thought Reasoning* | Wang et al. (ICLR 2023) | [arXiv:2203.11171](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.11171) | Majority voting for reliable outputs |
| **Corrective RAG** | *Corrective Retrieval-Augmented Generation (CRAG)* | Yan et al. (2024) | [arXiv:2401.15884](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.15884) | Direct blueprint for Assignment 2 CRAG |
| **Dense Passage Retrieval** | *Hypothetical Document Embeddings (HyDE)* | Gao et al. (2022) | [arXiv:2212.10496](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.10496) | Query rewriting optimization in Module 6 |
| **Agent Memory** | *Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning* | Shinn et al. (NeurIPS 2023) | [arXiv:2303.11366](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.11366) | Self-evaluating episodic memory |
| **Agent Memory Survey** | *A Survey on the Memory Mechanism of LLM-based Agents* | Zhang et al. (2024) | [arXiv:2404.13501](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.13501) | Comprehensive memory architecture taxonomy |
| **Agent Protocols** | *A Survey of AI Agent Protocols (MCP & A2A)* | Research Survey (2025) | [arXiv:2504.16736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.16736) | Open interoperability standards analysis |
